# CULINARA — AI-POWERED INTERACTIVE COOKBOOK

Build a complete, polished, hackathon-ready web application called **CULINARA**.

CULINARA is not a traditional recipe website and should NOT look like a normal food blog, recipe database, or generic AI chatbot.

It is an **immersive, interactive AI cookbook and personal culinary experience** that helps users decide what to cook based on their situation, preferences, ingredients, mood, weather, occasion, time, budget, and number of people.

The experience should feel like:

**a premium editorial cookbook + interactive storybook + modern restaurant website + playful game + AI kitchen assistant.**

The website should be visually spectacular, highly animated, detailed, tactile, and interactive while still being genuinely useful.

---

# 1. CORE PRODUCT IDEA

The central concept is:

> **“Tell CULINARA what your world looks like right now, and it creates what you should eat.”**

Instead of simply searching for:

> “pasta recipes”

the user can say:

> “It’s raining, I want something comforting, I have 30 minutes, I’m cooking for 3 people, I have $20, and I want Italian food.”

CULINARA should transform those inputs into a personalized culinary experience.

The AI should generate or recommend:

* Main dish
* Side dish
* Drink
* Dessert
* Ingredients
* Quantities
* Cooking time
* Difficulty
* Estimated cost
* Substitutions
* Nutrition information where possible
* Shopping list
* Cooking sequence

The interface should visually transform based on the user's choices.

---

# 2. IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

DO NOT make this look like:

* a dashboard
* a SaaS application
* a generic AI chatbot
* a standard recipe card
* a basic Bootstrap website
* a grid of rectangular cards
* a boring food delivery app

Avoid excessive conventional UI elements.

Instead, make the interface feel like a **living digital cookbook**.

Everything should feel tactile and alive.

Elements should move subtly.

Ingredients should have physical presence.

Transitions should feel intentional.

Pages should feel like they are being opened, unfolded, poured, stirred, or transformed.

---

# 3. VISUAL DIRECTION

**Luxury editorial cookbook × warm kitchen × modern interactive art × cinematic food photography × playful digital interface**

# 4. GLOBAL ANIMATION LANGUAGE

Animation is one of the most important parts of CULINARA.

Almost every meaningful interaction should have motion.

Use smooth, natural animations rather than random flashy effects.

Animations should feel physical.

Examples:

* lemons slowly roll across a surface
* basil leaves gently move
* steam rises from food
* spoons rotate slightly
* sauce drips
* flour particles scatter
* cards unfold like paper
* cookbook pages turn
* ingredients fly toward bowls
* maps morph into dishes
* food images gently zoom
* buttons react when hovered
* cards tilt slightly toward the cursor
* objects have subtle parallax
* transitions resemble moving through a physical kitchen

Use spring-like motion where appropriate.

Avoid excessive bouncing.

The goal is:

**“Everything feels alive.”**
